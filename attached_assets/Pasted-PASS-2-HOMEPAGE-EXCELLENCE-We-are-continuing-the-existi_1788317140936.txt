PASS 2 — HOMEPAGE EXCELLENCE

We are continuing the existing Westbridge International Academy (WIA) website.

The website is already functional and has already gone through a QA pass and a visual identity refinement. Do not rebuild the project from scratch. Do not change the technology stack. Do not replace working functionality.

Your job in this pass is to make the homepage feel exceptionally polished, editorial, premium, emotionally compelling, and worthy of a leading international school in Abuja, Nigeria.

PRIMARY GOAL

Transform the homepage from “excellent school website” into something that feels like it was designed by a high-end international digital design studio.

Think:

- prestigious international school
- sophisticated editorial design
- warm and human
- confident but understated
- premium without looking expensive for the sake of it
- distinctly African/Nigerian in atmosphere
- modern without becoming futuristic
- beautiful photography
- excellent typography
- generous whitespace
- strong visual rhythm
- subtle, purposeful interaction

Do NOT make it look like a SaaS website, startup landing page, generic AI website, or luxury hotel website.

---

1. HERO SECTION — MAKE THIS THE STRONGEST PART

Rework the hero composition without destroying the existing brand.

The hero should immediately communicate:

Westbridge International Academy
Where curiosity becomes capability.

The visitor should understand within seconds:

- this is a serious school
- it is international in outlook
- it is based in Abuja
- students are known, challenged, supported, and prepared for the future

Visual direction

Use a large, cinematic school image or carefully composed image treatment.

Prioritize imagery that feels:

- authentic
- Nigerian/African
- natural
- warm
- documentary/editorial
- diverse
- aspirational without being staged

Avoid obviously AI-looking or generic stock-school photography.

The hero should have strong visual hierarchy.

The headline should be the dominant typographic element.

Keep supporting text concise.

Make the primary CTA highly visible.

Suggested CTA hierarchy:

Explore Westbridge
Begin Your Journey

Avoid filling the hero with too many buttons, badges, statistics, or decorative elements.

Hero interaction

Add subtle motion only where it improves the experience:

- gentle image entrance
- subtle text reveal
- restrained movement while scrolling
- refined hover states

No excessive parallax.

No flashy animation.

Respect "prefers-reduced-motion".

---

2. NAVIGATION

Refine the existing navigation so it feels extremely polished.

Check:

- spacing
- typography
- logo treatment
- CTA button
- hover states
- active page state
- mobile navigation

The navigation should feel calm and confident.

Avoid excessive borders, shadows, pills, or glassmorphism.

If the existing announcement bar works well, preserve it but refine its spacing and typography.

---

3. HOMEPAGE STORYTELLING

The homepage should feel like a carefully constructed narrative rather than a collection of sections.

Create a natural story:

INTRODUCTION

Who Westbridge is.

↓

EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

How students learn and grow.

↓

ACADEMIC JOURNEY

What the student experience looks like from Early Years through Secondary.

↓

LIFE AT WESTBRIDGE

What it feels like to actually belong to the school.

↓

COMMUNITY

Students, teachers, parents, and Abuja.

↓

ADMISSIONS

A clear invitation to take the next step.

Each section should feel like it belongs to the same visual story.

---

4. IMPROVE THE "ABOUT" SECTION

Make the existing introduction/about section more editorial.

Avoid a generic:

"Welcome to our school" + paragraph + three cards.

Instead, create stronger visual hierarchy.

Use:

- large statement
- concise supporting copy
- strong photography
- intentional whitespace
- asymmetric or editorial composition where appropriate

The visitor should feel:

“These people genuinely care about education.”

---

5. ACADEMIC JOURNEY

Make the academic stages visually memorable.

Present:

- Early Years
- Primary
- Secondary

as part of one coherent educational journey.

Avoid making every stage look like an identical card.

Consider:

- alternating layouts
- large numbers
- photography
- editorial labels
- subtle dividers
- strong typography
- visual progression

The section should communicate progression and continuity.

---

6. "WHY WESTBRIDGE" SECTION

This section should communicate the school's strongest differentiators.

Prioritize ideas such as:

- academic excellence
- character and confidence
- individual attention
- international perspective
- technology and future readiness
- community
- wellbeing
- leadership

Do not simply create eight identical icon cards.

Instead, create visual hierarchy.

One or two ideas can be dominant, with supporting ideas arranged around them.

Keep the layout sophisticated and breathable.

---

7. CAMPUS / PLACE

Strengthen the sense that Westbridge exists in Abuja.

The visitor should feel a sense of place.

Use imagery and copy that suggest:

- Abuja's environment
- greenery
- spaciousness
- contemporary architecture
- community
- Nigerian identity
- calm and purposeful learning environments

Do not use stereotypical African visual clichés.

The African identity should feel natural and contemporary, not decorative.

---

8. STUDENT LIFE

Make this section emotionally engaging.

The visitor should be able to imagine a student actually living the Westbridge experience.

Show or communicate:

- friendships
- sports
- arts
- clubs
- leadership
- creativity
- collaboration
- discovery
- school traditions

Use photography as storytelling rather than decoration.

Avoid a repetitive grid of identical cards.

---

9. TESTIMONIAL

Make the parent/student testimonial feel authentic.

Use a strong editorial treatment.

Large quotation.

Small attribution.

Beautiful supporting image if appropriate.

Do not make it look like a review widget.

The testimonial should feel like part of the school's story.

---

10. NEWS & EVENTS

Keep this section clean and useful.

Prioritize:

- upcoming events
- school news
- important announcements

Avoid making it look like a generic blog grid.

Use strong typography and concise metadata.

---

11. FINAL ADMISSIONS CTA

The final CTA should feel like a natural conclusion to the entire homepage.

It should answer:

“What should I do next?”

Create a confident, warm closing section.

Possible messaging direction:

Your child's next chapter begins here.

Supporting copy should be concise.

Primary action:

Explore Admissions

Secondary action:

Book a Visit

Make this section visually memorable without becoming overly decorative.

---

12. TYPOGRAPHY

Review the entire homepage typography.

Improve:

- heading scale
- line height
- paragraph width
- hierarchy
- section labels
- button typography
- navigation typography

Use typography to create rhythm.

Large headlines should have room to breathe.

Avoid excessive bold text.

Avoid long paragraphs.

---

13. SPACING

This is extremely important.

Increase whitespace where necessary.

The homepage should never feel cramped.

Use consistent vertical rhythm between:

- section labels
- headings
- paragraphs
- buttons
- images
- content blocks

Premium design often comes from space and restraint, not decoration.

---

14. COLOR

Maintain the established Westbridge palette:

- deep forest green
- warm ivory
- muted gold
- charcoal / deep green
- subtle sage

Use gold sparingly.

Gold should feel like an accent, not the dominant color.

Avoid gradients unless they are extremely subtle and genuinely improve readability.

---

15. IMAGERY

Review every homepage image.

Prioritize a coherent photographic language.

Images should feel like they belong to the same school and the same world.

Prefer:

- natural expressions
- real-looking environments
- diverse Nigerian/African students
- authentic classroom moments
- outdoor learning
- sports
- arts
- teachers interacting naturally with students

Avoid:

- overly posed stock photography
- generic Western private-school imagery
- unrealistic architecture
- futuristic classrooms
- cheesy smiling-at-camera compositions

If the current image assets are weak, replace them with better appropriate assets where practical.

---

16. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Do not optimize only for desktop.

Inspect the homepage at:

- 1440px
- 1280px
- 1024px
- 768px
- 390px
- 375px

On mobile:

- preserve the visual hierarchy
- avoid oversized headings that create awkward wrapping
- ensure CTAs remain accessible
- prevent horizontal overflow
- maintain comfortable spacing
- ensure images crop beautifully
- make navigation excellent
- keep sections visually interesting without becoming excessively long

The mobile version should feel designed, not merely compressed.

---

17. ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain or improve:

- semantic HTML
- heading hierarchy
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus states
- sufficient contrast
- alt text
- accessible buttons
- accessible mobile navigation
- reduced-motion support

Do not sacrifice accessibility for visual effects.

---

18. PERFORMANCE

Do not introduce unnecessary heavy dependencies.

Avoid:

- excessive animation libraries
- huge image files
- unnecessary video backgrounds
- expensive effects
- duplicated components

Keep the homepage fast.

---

19. IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

DO:

- refine what already works
- preserve the existing architecture
- reuse existing components where appropriate
- create reusable components only when genuinely useful
- make deliberate visual improvements
- inspect the actual rendered website
- fix issues you discover

DO NOT:

- rebuild the entire application
- change frameworks
- replace the existing design system unnecessarily
- turn every section into cards
- add excessive gradients
- add glassmorphism
- add neon colors
- add excessive rounded corners
- add excessive animation
- add unnecessary badges
- add fake statistics
- add fake awards
- add fake accreditation
- add fake school claims
- add generic AI-generated marketing language
- break existing routes or functionality

---

20. FINAL QA — REQUIRED

After implementing the improvements:

1. Run the application.
2. Open the homepage in the browser.
3. Scroll through the entire page.
4. Inspect every section visually.
5. Test navigation.
6. Test every homepage CTA.
7. Test mobile navigation.
8. Test at 390px and 375px widths.
9. Check for horizontal scrolling.
10. Check browser console errors.
11. Check broken images.
12. Check broken links.
13. Check malformed JSX.
14. Check unused imports.
15. Check accessibility issues.
16. Check animation behavior.
17. Check "prefers-reduced-motion".
18. Run the production build if available.

IMPORTANT

Do not stop after editing files.

Actually inspect the rendered website and correct anything that looks wrong.

When finished, give me a concise report containing:

CHANGES MADE

- major visual improvements
- sections refined
- imagery changes
- responsive improvements
- interaction improvements

FILES CHANGED

List the main files modified.

QA RESULT

State whether:

- app runs successfully
- production build passes
- console is clean or what remains
- mobile was checked
- any known limitations remain

This is Pass 2 only. Keep the scope focused on homepage excellence.
